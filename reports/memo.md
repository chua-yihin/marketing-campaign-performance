# Memo: reallocate acquisition spend from Social to Display

**To:** Marketing team · **Data:** GA360 sample, 2016-08-01 to 2017-08-01 · **All spend figures are assumptions** (the sample has no cost data)

## 1. Recommendation

**Cut Social, scale Display (to 2x its current spend), and bank the rest: about +$120k in 90-day profit (range +$60k to +$180k).**

## 2. Why

- **CAC:** Social costs **$2,089** per first-time buyer and Display costs **$13**, against $64 across ranked channels. Social takes 66% of assumed spend ($119,084) for 57 buyers out of 198,473 new users.
- **ROI (90-day, 40% margin):** Social **-99%**, Display **+278%**, ranked-channel average -37%. Visit-to-purchase is 0.03% for Social vs 6.1% for Display (average 0.82%).
- **90-day retention:** 20.7% of Display users return, vs 3.0% for Social and 7.5% on average.

## 3. Expected impact

Remove all $119,084 of Social spend. Move $1,170 of it to Display, doubling that channel, at a marginal CAC 30% worse than its average ($17 instead of $13, to allow for diminishing returns). Bank the remaining $117,914.

**Range:** +$60k to +$180k (Social spend −50%/Display +50%, and the reverse). The gain is mostly Social's assumed spend banked, so its size is an assumption. The decision is not: Social breaks even only below **$0.005 per new user**, 122x under the assumed $0.60. Display is small (89 buyers), so growth is modest. Cutting Social holds in all 13 sensitivity scenarios. Scaling Display holds in 11; in 2, Organic Search ranks first.

## 4. Caveats

- **Assumed spend:** placeholders in `config/assumptions.yaml`.
- **First-touch attribution:** Social may assist other channels' conversions.
- **2016-17 data** from one store (Google Merchandise Store), which may not hold today.
- **Sign-up proxy:** `/registersuccess.html` (also fires on some logins); it does not affect CAC or ROI.
- **Upper-bound loss:** we assume every Social buyer is lost when the spend is cut.

## 5. Next step

Run a **4-week geo holdout**: pause Social and double Display in a random half of regions. Judge on **total revenue across all channels** vs control, which catches any assist Social gives other channels. First-touch Social buyers (about 6 in 4 weeks) are too few to measure directly. Proceed if total revenue is not significantly lower and Display's marginal CAC stays under its break-even of $50.
